Surface Approximation using Coons Patches

  • Engine: Unity WebGL
  • Languages: C#, JavaScript
  • Topic: Computational Geometry

Course: Computational Geometry
Author: Zotea Dumitru — Student ID: 40654A
University: University of Milan (UNIMI)

Description:
This project presents an interactive applet designed to study the approximation of surfaces through a rectangular grid of bilinear Coons patches. Starting from a reference surface defined over a planar domain, the system constructs a piecewise approximated surface by interpolating the boundary curves of each cell and reconstructing the interior through the classical Coons formulation.

The purpose of the application is not limited to visualizing a surface, but rather to analyze how the quality of the approximation changes with respect to domain discretization and surface complexity. The user can compare in real time the reference surface with its Coons-based approximation, evaluate numerical error metrics such as mean error, maximum error, and RMSE, and observe their spatial distribution through a heatmap.

The applet therefore serves as a didactic tool for exploring fundamental concepts in computational geometry and geometric modeling: boundary interpolation, piecewise parametric surface construction, continuity between adjacent patches, dependence of approximation error on discretization, and the distinction between exact boundary interpolation and approximate interior reconstruction.

Project Goals

  • study the construction of surfaces using bilinear Coons patches;
  • analyze the effect of domain subdivision on approximation quality;
  • compare smooth analytic surfaces with noise-based surfaces;
  • visualize and quantify approximation error in an interactive way.
